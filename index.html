AI mail summary

A private, single-user tool that creates a daily summary of the owner's own Gmail inbox.

  • Reads mail from the last 24 hours with read-only access (gmail.readonly).
  • Summarises it with a language model that runs locally on the owner's own computer.
  • Posts the summary to a chat server on the owner's own home network.

It is not offered to other users. See the privacy policy.
